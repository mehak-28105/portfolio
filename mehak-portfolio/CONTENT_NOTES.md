# Content and visual direction

## Résumé analysis

The strongest positioning is a full-stack engineer with applied AI workflow experience, financial-technology context and a practical product/design perspective—not an ML researcher. Microsoft establishes the AI/.NET/Azure story; Avira provides concrete React/Node/Firebase product work; FinBuddy connects the technical skill set to the degree's financial-technology focus. YaanBarpe and E-Cell support communication, design and collaboration rather than being inflated into extra software projects.

## Hierarchy

1. A brief engineering proposition, education context and direct work/contact actions.
2. Three selectable work stories: Microsoft internship work study, FinBuddy personal project, Avira internship work study.
3. Chronological internship experience with progressive disclosure.
4. About and education, followed by clearly categorized skills.
5. Leadership and a direct email contact.

## Editorial decisions

- One personal project is better than invented project volume. No fabricated demonstrations, repositories, client quotes, headshot or portfolio URLs.
- Employer details are limited to résumé-level information. Conceptual workflow/finance/HR visuals are newly drawn, labeled and do not represent confidential screens.
- Reported outcomes remain attributed to the résumé. FinBuddy's outcome percentages are not presented as independently audited performance.
- Exploring/learning tools stay separate from demonstrated tools; AWS Cloud Practitioner is not called a certification.
- Personal phone details are omitted. Email is the only external contact destination because no actual LinkedIn URL was embedded in the supplied PDF.
- No claim of current job availability is made; graduation remains 2027.

## Design rationale

Large serif headlines contrast with compact monospaced labels and a readable sans-serif body. The asymmetric hero, ruled sections and restrained palette suggest an editorial field notebook rather than a generic template. Code-native diagrams suit the evidence available and avoid stock imagery. Interaction is concentrated where it helps exploration, not on mandatory animations or distracting effects.
